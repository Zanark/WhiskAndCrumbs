## Implementation and safeguards

- `content\site.json`: explicit boolean `creamEffects`; absent/false disables markup.
- `web\cream-model.mjs`: reviewed model snapshot, byte-identical to the study.
- `web\cream-material.mjs`: original ambient material plus cursor-only deformation.
- `web\cream.mjs`: versioned dependency loading, production lifecycle and renderer.
- `web\cream.css`: decorative layers, readable content planes and pause control.
- `web\main.mjs` and the builder wire the enhancement without replacing baseline content.

The production GPU output is limited to about2M pixels and1.5DPR, with static
fallback below half-resolution. The30fpsceiling and one-frame scheduler prevent
unbounded work. The pointer uses a small scissored pass, copied above the page,
then the main canvas is restored to ambient-only state. No mouse locking, audio,
permission prompts or third-party requests are introduced.

`updateCursorShape` smooths velocity, direction, stretch and phase. `cursorWarp`
uses the same local-space deformation for the silhouette, dome shading and pink
tint; its centre avoids undefined `atan(0,0)`. Reciprocal axis scaling keeps the
head's area nearly constant. The crop allows the bounded1.51-radius extent.
`sampleProductionScene` doubles dimensions, adds seeded groups and the8-slot spawn
queue;`productionEdgeLayout` translates complete large-group envelopes to the edges.

The restored contact uses a shared smooth-union field for the scene, cursor and
trail. One background pass stays under the glass; a small scissored foreground
pass contributes only the cursor and local contact neck, not an opaque ambient
crop. Approaching shapes join and separate without moving background anchors.
Four precompiled programs share one WebGL context and buffer, with48primitive
slots and at most two draws per frame. Native/pause states remove contact and
restore the baseline pass. The local study remains separate and unchanged.

The local4174study and its source/artwork exports remain unchanged. Production
changes are separately allowlisted; no private manifests, masks or review files
are published.
