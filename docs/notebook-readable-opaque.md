The owner requested larger cake descriptions and titles, specifically including
"Pastel celebration cake", while keeping the now-approved button sizes. They also
rejected the translucent-looking attachment strips.

## Typography and paint

| Text at default browser settings | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cake-section description | 18px | 24px |
| Cake names and enlarged-photo titles | 20px | 28px |
| Open-book category title | 26px | 34px |
| Cover/static category titles | 32px | 36px |
| Notebook/photo button labels | 20px | 20px |

All sizes remain rem-based. Buttons retain their dimensions, padding, labels,
hit areas and exterior positioning. Helper text remains 18px.

The former attachment gradients mixed faint pastel with an opaque cream base,
creating a translucent-tape appearance. Faces and attachment strips now use the
same solid pastel pigment. Closed cover flags also change from 96% to fully
opaque pigment. Cream paper remains opaque; only the logical space outside the
physical paper and tabs stays transparent, preserving the protruding-tab outline.

Courgette/DM Sans, product names, catalogue membership and all 120 real media
variants are unchanged. So are every runtime module, the awning, entrance, level
camera/posters, brand assets and isolated 4174 experiment.
